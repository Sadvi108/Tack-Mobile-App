**Tack** — a careers app for students in Bangladesh.

Last updated: 6 September 2026

By using Tack you agree to what follows. It is short on purpose.

---

## What Tack is

An app that helps you work out what career to aim at, builds you a plan to get
there, shows you real job openings, and tells you how ready you are. It is free
to use.

## What Tack is not

**Tack is not a guarantee of a job.** The readiness score, the fit percentage
on an opening and the roadmap are all calculated from what you tell us and from
publicly listed jobs. They are a guide to help you decide what to do next, not
a prediction and not a promise. No employer is obliged to do anything because
of a number in this app.

**Tack does not employ anyone and does not recruit.** Openings in Radar come
from public job boards. We do not check that they are genuine, still open, or
fairly described, and we are not part of any application you make. If an
"employer" asks you for money, treat that as a warning sign and tell us.

**Tack is not careers advice from a qualified adviser**, and not legal,
financial or immigration advice.

## What you put into Tack

What you upload stays yours. You give us permission to store it and to process
it in order to run the features you use — to read your CV so it can be scored,
for example. Nothing more.

Do not upload anything that is not yours to upload, and do not upload other
people's personal information.

## The AI features

The coach, CV scoring, job-description analysis and interview practice use an
AI model, limited to three actions a day. **AI output can be wrong.** Check
anything that matters before acting on it. Your contact details are removed
before your text is sent to the model — see the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).

## Using it fairly

Do not attack the service, try to reach other students' data, scrape it, resell
it, or automate it beyond ordinary personal use. We may suspend an account that
does.
